# ISMS Communication Plan

| What | Who communicates | To whom | When | How |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Information security policy | CISO | All staff; public on request | On approval; annually | Intranet, website |
| Topic-specific policies | Policy owner | Affected staff | On change | Intranet, training |
| ISMS performance and risks | CISO | Top management | Monthly; management review | Committee meeting |
| Security incidents (internal) | Incident manager | Stakeholders per ISMS-POL-004 | During incident | Incident channel |
| Personal data breaches | DPO | Supervisory authority; data subjects | Within 72 h (GDPR Art. 33) / without undue delay | Authority portal; email |
| Significant incidents (NIS2, if applicable) | CISO | CSIRT / competent authority | 24 h early warning; 72 h notification; 1 month final report | National portal |
| Security incidents (customers) | Account owner + CISO | Affected customers | As per contract | Email |
| Contact with authorities (5.5) | CISO | Police, CSIRT, regulator | As needed | Contact list in {{LOCATION}} |
| Special interest groups (5.6) | CISO | ISACs, professional forums | Ongoing | Membership |
| Certification status | CEO / Sales | Customers, prospects | On change | Trust page |
