# Resources, Competence and Awareness

## 1. Resources (7.1)
Top management provides ISMS resources through the annual budget. Resource needs are reviewed at management review. Current allocation: {{FTE_AND_BUDGET}}.

## 2. Competence (7.2)
> **Mandatory documented information:** evidence of competence.

| Role | Required competence | How acquired | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| CISO / ISMS manager | ISO/IEC 27001 implementation, risk management | Lead Implementer training, experience | Certificate, CV |
| Internal auditor | ISO/IEC 27001 and ISO 19011 audit techniques | Internal/Lead Auditor training | Certificate |
| Engineers | Secure coding, cloud security | Annual secure development training | Training platform records |
| Incident responders | Incident handling | Tabletop exercises | Exercise reports |
| All staff | Security awareness | Onboarding + annual training | Training records |

Competence records are kept in {{HR_SYSTEM}}. Gaps found during reviews are closed through training, mentoring or reassignment, and the effectiveness of the action is evaluated.

## 3. Awareness (7.3, control 6.3)
All persons doing work under the organization's control are made aware of:
- the information security policy
- their contribution to the ISMS's effectiveness, including the benefits of improved performance
- the implications of not conforming to ISMS requirements

| Activity | Audience | Frequency | Evidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Onboarding security briefing + policy acknowledgement | New joiners | Before system access | Signed acknowledgement |
| Security awareness e-learning | All | Annually | Completion report |
| Phishing simulation | All | Quarterly | Simulation report |
| Role-based training (developers, admins) | Technical staff | Annually | Completion report |
| Security newsletter / updates | All | Monthly | Archive |

## 4. People controls (Annex A 6)
- **6.1 Screening:** background checks before employment, proportional to role and classification of information accessed and in line with local law.
- **6.2 Terms and conditions:** employment contracts include information security responsibilities.
- **6.4 Disciplinary process:** a formal process for policy violations, communicated to all staff.
- **6.5 After termination:** confidentiality obligations continue after leaving. Leaver checklist: revoke access and return assets.
- **6.6 NDAs:** confidentiality agreements for staff, contractors and third parties, reviewed periodically.
