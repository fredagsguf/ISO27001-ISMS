# Information Security Policy

> **Mandatory documented information** (clause 5.2). Communicate it within the organization and make it available to interested parties as appropriate.

## 1. Purpose
Information is critical to {{ORG_NAME}}, its customers and its partners. This policy states top management's commitment to protecting it. It also sets the framework for the information security management system (ISMS).

## 2. Scope
This policy applies to all employees, contractors and third parties who access {{ORG_NAME}} information or systems within the ISMS scope (ISMS-DOC-002).

## 3. Information security principles
We protect information according to its value and risk:
- **Confidentiality.** Only authorized persons can access information.
- **Integrity.** Information is accurate and complete, and only changed in authorized ways.
- **Availability.** Information is available to authorized users when they need it.

## 4. Commitments of top management
Top management commits to:
1. Set information security objectives that are consistent with the strategic direction (see ISMS-DOC-004).
2. Satisfy applicable requirements related to information security, including legal, regulatory and contractual obligations.
3. Continually improve the ISMS.
4. Provide the resources needed to establish, maintain and improve the ISMS.
5. Integrate information security requirements into business processes.

## 5. Framework
- Information security risks are identified, assessed and treated according to ISMS-PRC-001.
- Controls are selected using ISO/IEC 27001 Annex A and recorded in the Statement of Applicability.
- Topic-specific policies (`policies/`) give more detailed rules. They are mandatory for everyone in scope.
- Roles and responsibilities are defined in ISMS-DOC-003.

## 6. Responsibilities
- **Everyone** follows this policy and the topic-specific policies, and reports security events and weaknesses immediately (see ISMS-POL-004).
- **Managers** make sure their teams know and follow the policies.
- **The CISO / ISMS manager** maintains the ISMS and reports on its performance to top management.

## 7. Compliance
Violations can lead to disciplinary action (control 6.4) and, where applicable, legal action. Exceptions must be requested from the CISO, risk-assessed, approved and time-limited.

## 8. Review
This policy is reviewed at least annually and after significant changes.
