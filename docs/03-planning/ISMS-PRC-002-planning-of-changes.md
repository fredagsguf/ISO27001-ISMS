# Planning of Changes to the ISMS

## 1. Purpose
When the ISMS needs to change, the change is carried out in a planned manner (clause 6.3).

## 2. Triggers
Examples: changes in scope, organization structure, key suppliers, regulation, technology platforms, results of risk assessment, audits, incidents, or management review decisions.

## 3. Process
1. **Raise.** Log the proposed change (pull request to this repository and/or a ticket in {{TICKETING_SYSTEM}}).
2. **Assess.** The CISO assesses the purpose and possible consequences, the effect on the ISMS's integrity, the resources needed, and the reallocation of responsibilities.
3. **Risk.** Update the risk assessment if the change affects information security risks (ISMS-PRC-001).
4. **Approve.** Approval at the level matching the impact: CISO for minor changes, top management for scope or policy changes.
5. **Implement and communicate.** Update documents (ISMS-PRC-003), train affected people, and communicate (ISMS-DOC-006).
6. **Review.** Check the effect of the change at the next information security committee meeting. Review unintended changes and act to limit adverse effects (8.1).

Changes to information processing facilities and systems follow the operational change management process (control 8.32, ISMS-POL-008).
