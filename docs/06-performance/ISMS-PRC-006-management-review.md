# Management Review Procedure

> **Mandatory documented information:** evidence of the results of management reviews (9.3.3).

## 1. Frequency and participants
At least **annually**, and before the certification audit. Chaired by {{CEO}}, with the CISO and the leadership team.

## 2. Required inputs (9.3.2)
Use this agenda. Every item must be covered and minuted.
- [ ] a) Status of actions from previous management reviews
- [ ] b) Changes in external and internal issues relevant to the ISMS (including climate change, per Amd 1:2024)
- [ ] c) Changes in needs and expectations of interested parties relevant to the ISMS
- [ ] d) Feedback on information security performance, including trends in:
  - [ ] nonconformities and corrective actions
  - [ ] monitoring and measurement results (ISMS-PRC-004)
  - [ ] audit results
  - [ ] fulfilment of information security objectives (ISMS-DOC-004)
- [ ] e) Feedback from interested parties
- [ ] f) Results of risk assessment and status of the risk treatment plan
- [ ] g) Opportunities for continual improvement

## 3. Required outputs (9.3.3)
- Decisions on continual improvement opportunities
- Any need for changes to the ISMS (feeds ISMS-PRC-002)
- Resource decisions, and actions with owners and due dates

## 4. Minutes template
```
Management Review — {{DATE}}
Attendees:
Inputs reviewed: a) … g) (attach evidence)
Decisions:
Actions: | ID | Action | Owner | Due |
Approved by:
```
Minutes are stored in {{RECORDS_LOCATION}} and referenced from the next review.
