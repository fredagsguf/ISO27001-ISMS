# Acceptable Use Policy

## 1. Scope
All personnel and third parties who use {{ORG_NAME}} information, devices, accounts or networks.

## 2. Rules
1. Use company information and systems only for authorized business purposes. Limited personal use is allowed if it does not affect security or productivity.
2. Do not share accounts or authentication information. Never give your password or MFA codes to anyone, including IT.
3. Do not install unapproved software or browser extensions. Do not disable security tools such as EDR, disk encryption or MDM.
4. Store company information only in approved systems ({{APPROVED_STORAGE}}). Do not use personal email or personal cloud storage for company data.
5. Do not paste Confidential or Restricted information into unapproved external services, including generative AI tools not listed in {{APPROVED_AI_TOOLS}}.
6. **Clear desk and clear screen (7.7):** lock your screen when you leave it, and do not leave Confidential documents unattended.
7. **Remote working (6.7):** use approved devices, keep the device up to date, avoid shared or public screens, and use secure networks or the company VPN where required.
8. **Off-premises assets (7.9):** never leave devices unattended in public. Report loss or theft immediately.
9. **Endpoint devices (8.1):** company devices are managed (MDM), encrypted, and patched automatically. Personal devices (BYOD) can access company data only through {{BYOD_SOLUTION}}.
10. **Return of assets (5.11):** return all company assets and information when you leave or your role changes.
11. Report suspected security events immediately to {{SECURITY_CONTACT}} (see ISMS-POL-004).

## 3. Monitoring
To protect information, the organization may monitor the use of its systems in line with applicable law and the privacy notice for employees.

## 4. Acknowledgement
All personnel acknowledge this policy before they get access, and every year after that.
